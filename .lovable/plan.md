# TheHyped full visual redesign

## Goal
Redesign the entire publication while preserving its pages, editorial content, working links, and review structure.

## Locked visual direction
- Ink-and-cyan palette: #080D10, #142026, #19C3C8, #E9F1F2.
- Space Mono for headlines and Rubik for body text.
- Magazine-grid front page with a clear lead story, briefs, rankings, category desks, and newsletter placement.

## Scope
- Replace the current visual system across the header, footer, homepage, indexes, categories, reviews, comparison, forms, and legal pages.
- Establish consistent typography, spacing, borders, imagery treatment, buttons, scores, tables, and article hierarchy.
- Keep existing routes and sample editorial content intact.
- Preserve accessible navigation, mobile behavior, metadata, and structured data.
- Verify representative pages on desktop and mobile after implementation.

## Technical notes
- Implement the chosen direction through semantic tokens in the shared stylesheet and reusable site components.
- Keep the current file-based content model; no hosting, domain, or live email service work is included.
