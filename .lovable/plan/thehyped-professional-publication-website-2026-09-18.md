# TheHyped professional publication website

## Goal
Build TheHyped as a premium, trustworthy technology publication for AI tools, business software, and finance apps. The first release will be a polished, responsive editorial website; domain, hosting, live newsletter delivery, analytics, and affiliate tracking remain for a later phase.

## Visual direction
- Use the selected **Redline** palette: near-black, warm off-white, decisive red, and cobalt accents.
- Use **Sora** for headings and **Manrope** for body text.
- Build a **magazine front page** with a commanding lead review, ranked recommendations, comparison highlights, category desks, and a newsletter band.
- Take the reference template’s bold digital-business character as inspiration, while creating an original, more editorial and credible result.
- Use custom product-style visuals and restrained motion so the publication feels premium rather than like a generic AI landing page.

## Pages and navigation
Create working, shareable pages for:
- Home
- Reviews index and a complete sample review
- AI Tools
- Business Software
- Finance Apps
- Compare index and a complete sample comparison
- Blog
- Newsletter
- About and Contact
- Disclosure, Privacy Policy, and Terms

The shared header will prioritize AI Tools, Business Software, Finance Apps, Reviews, Compare, and Newsletter. The footer will carry company and legal links.

## Editorial experience
- Populate the site with credible demonstration content clearly presented as editorial samples, not fabricated user testimonials or unverifiable claims.
- Create reusable article cards, score treatments, price labels, verdict summaries, breadcrumbs, related-reading blocks, and disclosure labels.
- Build the sample review with the PDF’s full structure: verdict, audience/price/rating, visual demo, problem, features, pros/cons, pricing, alternatives, final verdict, and CTA.
- Build the sample comparison with summary, feature breakdown, pricing, who should choose each tool, FAQs, and final verdict.
- Ensure each review links to its category and related content, and each comparison links to both reviewed products.

## Quality and discoverability
- Give every page unique search and social metadata.
- Add accessible hierarchy, keyboard-friendly navigation, mobile menu behavior, visible focus states, and readable contrast.
- Add structured data for the organization, breadcrumbs, articles, reviews, software products, and FAQs where appropriate.
- Include a sitemap-friendly route structure and retain a fast, lightweight implementation.
- Verify the finished website visually on desktop and mobile, including menus, links, forms, layout stability, and error-free loading.

## Technical notes
- Use the existing React/TanStack application and its semantic design-token system.
- Keep content file-based for this phase, matching the PDF’s long-term-control approach without requiring a database.
- Forms will have polished validation and honest “coming soon” submission states until newsletter/email services are selected.
- Generate and store original visual assets inside the project; do not reuse or hotlink assets from the reference website.
